# Cinematic look development: establish the photographed world

Read for professional film/television, premium live-action drama or photographic recreation before
requesting scene images and video. Keep photographic decisions in VISUAL, accepted media
in ASSETS and shot requirements in STORYBOARD; Treatment explains the target.
The RunningHub H3-only video route and source fidelity remain authoritative.

Use [aesthetic direction](aesthetic-direction.md) to connect photographic choices to the
scene's viewer position, performance and meaning. Source/hero comparison supports look but
does not replace the complete dynamic/sound unit or acceptance of its emotional function.

## Define a reference-backed target

Use accessible reference passages for the intended photography, including the source film when
recreating it. A named title, director, camera brand or cinematic adjective is not observed evidence.
If a benchmark cannot be viewed, mark its traits unknown and use actual supplied evidence or a
reviewable proposed direction. A previous merely acceptable project is not automatically the new
quality standard. Separate source observations, inferred technique and target production choices.

Record which photographic qualities should persist, which may change per scene, and what would
count as failure. Broad comedy, graphic blush, dramatic makeup and stylized reactions can belong
inside a photographed world; do not make acting restrained merely to obtain visual realism.

Photographic integration concerns how the image is rendered, not whether its story events are
possible in ordinary life. Designed effects, expressive light and unusual costume/makeup can
share credible scale, occlusion, material response and light interaction with performers. Avoiding
an unwanted game-asset or plastic appearance is not a ban on CG/VFX production. Evaluate the
chosen effect's narrative contribution and integration; use
[scoped exclusions](../production/reference-scopes.md#先写用途排除项按需)
instead of declaring all assets effect-free for stability.

## Use professional responsibilities as decisions, not extra agents

| Responsibility | Concrete decision and test |
| --- | --- |
| Director/performance | Relationship, attention and source register; behavior reads at the selected shot size |
| Art/costume/makeup | Location layers, objects and surfaces, wardrobe construction and character-specific grooming; a coherent world rather than unrelated attractive assets |
| Photography | Viewpoint, perspective, focus, light motivation, exposure relationships and camera behavior; the observable result supports the scene |
| Generation | Actual references, supported H3 operation/configuration and feasible temporal load; adopted output realizes those choices |
| Compositing/color | Shared photographic space, edges/contact/occlusion, shot balance and intentional grade; inspect the processed output |
| Sound/editorial | Voice, acoustic perspective, contact cues, silence, score and cutting rhythm; listen to the finished scene |

One agent/person can fulfill these responsibilities. No six-agent review grid, extra per-prompt
approval or unrelated production system is required. Carry settled choices and budget authority.

## Build a concise photographic brief per scene

First resolve the global [style agreement](style-lock.md#persistent-and-scene-specific-choices),
then describe this scene's variation. Keep a clear progression in existing production records:
global direction -> scene art/photography -> accepted integrated images -> representative H3
performance and adjacent cuts -> batch -> finishing/export. Reuse evidence that remains applicable.
Each handoff carries its media/decision version, unresolved critical conditions and actual request
or finishing destination. Independent settled work may proceed while another scene is unresolved;
do not scale an unresolved direction or label a provisional test accepted.

Describe the choices that actually shape the image, avoiding a checklist of decorative camera terms.

| Dimension | What to specify when relevant | What to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light motivation | Physical source, world-space direction, apparent softness, falloff and reflection; local changes as actors move | Face/body/background share the light; shadow direction and transition agree |
| Exposure and hierarchy | What leads attention; intended face/background brightness, highlight treatment and readable shadow detail | Unintended blown skin/cloth or crushed costume detail does not obscure the performance |
| Space/perspective | Camera height/side/distance, depth layers, foreground and relationship geometry | People inhabit a location instead of looking pasted against a backdrop |
| Focus/optics | Readable subject plane, needed background evidence, selective blur or deep focus, meaningful focus changes | Blur follows attention and depth; key listeners/props remain readable |
| Material response | Skin/makeup sheen, hair edges, fabric weave/folds, object roughness/reflection under this light | Materials differ credibly and survive angle/movement without waxy or plastic homogenization |
| Color and environment | Scene palette carried by real surfaces and costumes; light color and background activity | Color supports attention and atmosphere; no unexplained saturation/temperature reset |
| Camera/motion | Stillness or support/path/pace/landing, subject motion and expected clarity or blur | Camera observes the scene with understandable movement and consistent temporal texture |

World light projects differently into reverse angles. Do not copy a fixed screen-left key into every
view. Negative fill, rim light, practicals or diffusion are optional tools chosen for an observed
result, not compulsory film markers. Beauty and credible photography can coexist; do not add scars,
blemishes, dirt or heavy pores to prove realism or change an accepted face. Avoid importing a
neutral identity-sheet gaze or studio pose into a scene unless its story calls for that behavior.

Use lens/camera words as shorthand with an observable intention, not a hardware claim. Exact focal
length, shutter angle, f-stop, light ratio or Kelvin value is not recoverable merely from a compressed
frame. Only set numerical parameters where chosen and supported. No universal 35mm, 24fps, deep
shadows, shallow depth of field, anamorphic flare, grain or low saturation requirement applies.

## Make art and materials belong to the scene

Resolve the visible construction in ASSETS and scene records before asking for photographic polish:

- Location depth, circulation and useful set dressing support blocking, sightlines and the story's
  social world. Preserve the source's relevant background activity; do not add decorative clutter
  or remove extras simply to make a cleaner image.
- Costume layers, fit, thickness, seams and accessories agree with accepted looks and movement.
  Hair/makeup and prop scale agree across views. Add wetness, wear or dirt only when owned by story
  state or the agreed design; surface damage is not a prerequisite for realism.
- Describe relevant differences in skin, fabric, metal, glass and environment response under the
  actual light: sheen, softness, fold behavior, transparency or reflection. Do not ask every surface
  to share the same glossy finish or add excessive skin detail that changes the accepted face.
- At contact and overlap, people/props have readable support, occlusion and contact shadows or
  reflections where expected. Furniture, costume and held objects react to motion within the
  intended physical/stylized world. Inspect integrated shot images, not isolated asset beauty alone.

If art, lighting or geometry is incoherent in the still, fix that owner before adding texture,
shallow focus or final filters. Link the accepted scene/shot image to the relevant asset/look versions.

## Resolve look in actual scene images before batching motion

Use representative tests for this work: face/performance readability, a main location/relationship,
and a demanding lighting/contact or moving passage when needed. There is no required image count.
Identity sheets are masters; accepted scene/shot images integrate identity, current state, light,
space and materials at one declared boundary. A grey portrait alone does not establish the scene's
photography. A layout/low-poly previs controls geometry, not the final photographic look.

Judge look candidates at intended viewing size and inspect relevant regions at native resolution.
Compare with chosen reference evidence at a comparable scene/shot, not arbitrary differently exposed
frames. The selected hero images establish accepted visual targets; mark unresolved directions rather
than assume beautiful means accepted. A style image cannot alter identity, story geography or reveal
permission. [Reference scopes](../production/reference-scopes.md) owns those boundaries.

For the requested professional look, do not start a full video batch while representative scene images
have unresolved critical problems in light, integration, focus, material or photographic hierarchy.
Asset/look exploration may use already authorized accounts/budget; this rule does not grant new spend.
Reuse suitable accepted evidence rather than generate a redundant test package for every revision.

## Plan real coverage and preserve native detail

Generate the close-up/insert/reverse view the scene actually needs with matching camera/light/state,
instead of routinely creating it by greatly magnifying a small region of a wide shot. Document source
dimensions, crop region, output dimensions and intended use. Evaluate the actual scale-up at delivery
size; pixel arithmetic quantifies enlargement, not quality. A crop is acceptable when its detail and
composition survive the agreed target. Upscaling cannot certify lost native detail or a new lens view.

When an exact opening matters, use the accepted shot image through a verified first-frame operation
if compatible with required inputs. A generic composition reference is guidance, not a locked opening.
For either route, compare the real generated opening with the accepted visual target. Do not invent
first-frame/audio/quality settings absent from the actual RunningHub app.

## Test that the look survives time

Before scaling a new photographic direction, inspect a representative H3 passage containing the
actual risk: speech, head turn, contact, occlusion or camera movement. Compare opening, changing and
settled phases and play normally when possible. Static approval cannot establish motion approval.

For physical action, check support and weight transfer, contact -> consequence, acceleration or
hesitation, release and settling where relevant. Hands/feet should not slide, pass through objects
or manipulate weightless props unintentionally. Clothing and hair respond without independent
texture crawling. Judge against the source's timing and register: comic exaggeration or stylized
physics can be intentional. [Shot blocks](../production/shot-blocks.md#direct-legible-action-and-camera-geometry)
owns translating the necessary phases into executable direction.

Include the relevant neighbor cut and actual sound in the representative test when available.
Use [scene sound](../playbooks/craft/sound-mix.md#plan-drama-sound-before-generation) to test voice,
contact cues, acoustic continuity and quiet. Allocate effort to demonstrated risks; fixed
40/30/20/10 splits and sound-as-half-the-picture claims are not production or acceptance rules.

Check face/material consistency, hair/fabric shimmer, edge halos, changing highlights/shadows,
focus-plane stability, unintended exposure pulses, believable contact and camera/subject movement.
Separate deliberate stylized expression or lighting from generation defects. Do not solve texture
drift by freezing necessary acting or solve contact by silently replacing the source action.

If the input image is already weak, repair art/photography first. If it is strong but motion degrades,
inspect reference roles, load, operation and the app's verified configuration; change one implicated
factor within the agreed budget and keep source obligations. Record app/version, input versions,
resolution, returned dimensions and exposed generation settings. Do not blame quantization or prescribe
sampling values without evidence. If a necessary look remains unattainable, record the limit and
keep the result provisional instead of lowering the standard retrospectively.

## Accept evidence, not adjectives

Add photographic conditions and results to the existing scene/candidate record:

| Target/evidence | Observable condition | Static result | Motion result | Neighbor/finishing result | Repair owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source or accepted visual target and exact media version | Light/space/focus/material/color/motion obligation | pass / fail / unverified | pass / fail / unverified | pass / fail / unverified | Art, shot design, inputs, H3 or finishing |

Critical failed/unverified conditions block full cinematic acceptance; partial previews can still
show their documented limits. A structural checker or image similarity score cannot award this
quality pass. Recheck affected gates after recropping, retiming, matte replacement, grading or export.
Final [finishing](../production/post-finishing.md) and actual listening remain required.
