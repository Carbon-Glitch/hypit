# Fidelity contract: 体验保真、形式自由、改编增值

Read for reference-led recreation, cloning or localization before Treatment, asset planning and
prompt drafting. Original creative work uses relevant references selectively; it does not inherit
a requirement to copy every reference. This contract extends fidelity beyond plot-point coverage.

## Establish the experience and change boundary

[Strict content conservation](content-conservation.md) governs this installation: all indexed creative
contributions, not only critical plot functions, must be retained or non-weaker replaced; additions
are separate. No_loss persists in reimagining and approved original revisions. Counts alone cannot
offset an absent item, and shrinking director/audience paragraphs into generic labels is not fidelity.

Default short-drama recreation protects curiosity, humor, anticipation, tension, reversal impact
and emotional residue, while allowing cast design, world, props, camera and effects to be
redesigned. Creative improvements serving those experiences are already in scope. Describe
each source mechanism, concrete target replacement and its observable condition; test each
experience separately. Form preservation applies when the user explicitly requires that form,
not as an automatic veto on a better target design. Preserve every experience-bearing subtle
detail as an obligation or testable replacement rather than only preserving plot points.

Record in Brief/Treatment what the user wants changed and what should remain. For explicitly literal
recreation, unmentioned production-relevant details inherit from the source: performed
movement and expressions, intensity, reaction coverage, cut purpose, stylized effects, sound and
atmosphere. Language/casting/location changes do not imply toned-down acting, realistic effects,
slower pacing or a new genre. Do not equate Western/localized with restrained, realistic or serious.

Select the scope modes in [adaptation and dialogue](adaptation-and-dialogue.md). A casting/world/language
change normally keeps a bounded adaptation; "professional optimization" alone is not an instruction
to replace its core exchanges, premise contrast or genre. Explicitly commissioned reimagining remains
available. Log consequential wording and mechanism changes, not merely a theme-equivalence claim.

For default experience-led recreation, redesign or localization, inherit essential experience and causal obligations,
while surface form and technique can change within the Brief. Before drafting, read the
[director blueprint](director-blueprint.md): document which concrete source mechanism produces
each experience and how its replacement should preserve or strengthen it. A difficulty with H3
does not authorize a downgrade. Judge every proposed substitution on three axes:

| Axis | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Story function | Does it preserve information, relationships, causality and setup/payoff? |
| Observable form | What source device creates the experience; what is retained or deliberately replaced, and on what basis? |
| Expressive effect | Does it preserve intensity, comic contrast, tension, tenderness, rhythm and the viewer's attention? |

The same story function alone is insufficient. Different interactions may establish the same power
relation but create different pleasures and feelings. In experience-led adaptation, new forms can
be the answer when the essential response and consequence remain legible. Compare each essential
experience separately; an unrelated gain cannot excuse a loss. A written rationale cannot prove
equivalence: preparation tests the design, actual viewing tests realization, and audience feedback
when available can challenge the predicted response.

Routine form changes and improvements within this experience-led Brief proceed normally, including
distinctive actions, props, shots or effects. Record their before/after mechanisms and basis in
Treatment; do not request fresh permission merely because the form changed. If a change would
materially alter the core requested experience, relationship or an explicitly retained form and
is not already authorized, make concrete alternatives reviewable and request that decision. Do not label feasibility-driven weakening
as localization or hide it in negative prompts.

Use [source audio policy](../production/source-audio-policy.md) for an explicitly selected
subtitle/visual-first commission. Preserve all established obligations and record unheard source
sound as unknown; target sound is newly authored, not a reconstructed source fact. This changes
the declared reading scope, not known-content conservation or actual target listening. It cannot
support a full source-sound-fidelity claim until that modality is genuinely compared.

## Keep source evidence independent of target drafting

Read the actual source and create its detail catalogue before target redesign. SOURCE_SHOTS/SOURCE_CONTEXT own
observations; SOURCE_CONTEXT owns interpretations. The machine-readable catalogue, when used, is a frozen
export of those observations, not a fresh list emitted from the adapted job manifest. Each detail
has a stable ID, source time, evidence, concrete observation and importance. SOURCE_CONTEXT links
those details in [experience units](reference-events.md#cross-shot-experience-units); their
ordered relationships and later payoff need explicit target conditions too. Preserve the original
wording/history when correcting it; a legitimate new observation invalidates affected allocation
and reviews. A source review compares the records with original media, not with the adapted plan.
Compare source observations with the actual original during the same analysis pass. Correct gaps in
SOURCE_SHOTS/SOURCE_CONTEXT and propagate only affected consumers. No independent reviewer,
waiver, separate reading report or frozen allocation is required.

Before target writing, compare the catalogue against the complete scoped source and its neighbors for
attractions omitted from all selected entries, including gaps between their intervals. Short
exchanges, active listeners, character contrasts, hope before reversal, title passages, sound and
expressive pauses can be essential despite adding little synopsis information. Record their
observation, contribution and scoped disposition at the existing owners. A fully allocated but
incomplete catalogue cannot establish fidelity. See [dramatic craft](dramatic-craft.md).

Classify critical, supporting and incidental details by their contribution to this work, not solely
by whether they are named in a plot synopsis. Gait, mouth shape, an exaggerated reaction, a beat of
silence or a music/effect hit may carry the premise. Do not demote difficult details to incidental
just because the model failed. Importance prioritizes review; it does not authorize omission of any
indexed creative contribution. Unrelated technical pixels/background blinks need not enter the creative
inventory, but that scope judgment happens against the source during source analysis, not after failure.

## Preserve performance and atmosphere as sequences

Capture the source's appearance and behavior as actually observed, including broad comedy, stylized
reactions and graphic effects when present. Write stimulus, onset, peak, hold and release only at
the precision the evidence supports. Track listeners and bystanders as well as the speaking actor.
A small face in a wide shot does not replace a source close-up whose purpose is a readable reaction.

Record how atmosphere develops: palette/light, material and background activity, vocal energy,
acoustic space, music pulse, designed effects, pauses and editing acceleration. Describe its concrete
contributors and their temporal relationships instead of merely naming an emotional genre.

For every indexed creative detail, define an observable target condition: what must be visible/audible,
from what shot size/angle, for which action phase, relative to which trigger, and what would count
as weakened or missing. Distinguish uncertainty from an intentional change.

## Carry detail through all production stages

| Stage | Fidelity gate |
| --- | --- |
| Source reading | Whole source inspected; every shot indexed; critical phases and sound verified or marked unresolved |
| Adaptation | Source catalogue compared with target decisions on function, mechanism and effect; permitted changed forms have testable replacements, no unrecorded downgrades |
| Assets/pre-vis | Identity plus needed acting, contact, focus, atmosphere and effect references; shot compositions readable |
| H3 request | Detail-level targets reach actual prompt/input/post instructions; all relevant prohibitions checked for conflict |
| Generated candidates | Source-versus-output comparison of each critical target condition and its experience, accounting for permitted form changes |
| Edit/finishing | Setup, action, payoff and reaction survive the selected intervals; source effects and sound cues executed |
| Delivery | Inspect actual export with sound; each critical detail has passed viewing evidence or disclosed unresolved status |

The RunningHub H3-only video restriction remains in force. When a difficult detail fails, improve
compositions, references, action grouping, shot size or editing within that route and the agreed
budget. Pilot the hardest contact/acting passage before batch generation where needed. A model
limit must remain visible; a checker pass cannot establish the limit has been solved.
