# Director blueprint: carry experience into performed picture and sound

Use for narrative scene design, written coverage, H3 drafting and repairs. DESIGN's director section or existing Treatment owns interpretation/change decisions. For ordinary recreation, STORYBOARD owns complete story/words as well as shots, process, cues and their experience/detail IDs; when a full literary body is needed, it owns story/words and the storyboard expands it. No second screenplay or extra provider format is created.

New directing prose, scene coverage, cue design and H3 creative language belong to the
[current-Agent writing workflow](document-writing.md). GPT supplies observed source evidence and actual
asset/animatic feedback, then executes the accepted blueprint; it cannot rewrite performance or
dialogue during compilation. Group related scene revisions in that same session.

## Contract the experience, allow its form to change

[No-loss conservation](content-conservation.md) protects every indexed director/audience/plot/
dialogue/shot-language contribution. Director-intent and audience-experience prose may expand or
change form with an explicit non-weaker counterpart; never collapse it to a generic theme label.
The actual visual/sound unfolding must also survive assets, request, chosen intervals and final cut.

First apply [adaptation and dialogue](adaptation-and-dialogue.md) to lock change intensity and
ordinary comprehension; [attraction and rhythm](attention-and-rhythm.md) develops the audience's
changing question, contrast, strategy and active quiet. Detailed director-intent prose is planning
information, not an instruction to make every action slower or every hold longer.

For adaptation read the actual [multidimensional source-shot rows](source-shot-analysis.md)
and their SH/E details alongside [source experience units](reference-events.md#cross-shot-experience-units).
Each target row links the contributing source shots/events and resolves their acting/listener phases,
information release, spatial/prop state, camera/cut and sound obligations. Splitting or merging shots
changes carriers, not the inherited obligations; do not reduce the rich source reading to a synopsis. For original work derive the units from Brief and authored scene. Each essential unit has an entering viewer/character position, necessary setup, change/reveal, reaction and aftermath. A synopsis, an emotion adjective or correct dialogue is insufficient.

State the target mechanism and what actual viewing would disprove it. Forms can change and gain clarity, humor or richer expression; core experiences remain individually testable and explicitly requested literal details remain bound. Do not claim private original intent or universal response. New spectacle cannot compensate for an omitted relationship beat or reversal consequence.

Before expanding coverage, settle the [aesthetic/directing choice](aesthetic-direction.md):
viewer position, subtext and an organizing image/sound relationship. Link the unit's
[whole-work contribution](source-deconstruction.md#从解构承接到制作和成片): which choice, cost,
knowledge, relation, motif or selected improvement it advances. Resolve show/withhold conditions
across picture, text and sound; inherit selected target grammar/variation, with source camera
forms binding only when explicitly retained. An accepted design is an input, not a reason to
reinvent the scene for every prompt.

Use [storyboard craft](storyboard-craft.md) for the thirteen relevant directing dimensions
and dialogue/action/exposition alternatives. Select what this scene needs, then resolve the
existing row below. The guide is a choice aid, not another thirteen-column form or pass score.

## Build the integrated shot row

Each consequential STORYBOARD row or linked detail card resolves:

| Relationship | Decision needed for execution |
| --- | --- |
| Experience / dependency | Unit and detail IDs; required setup, viewer/character knowledge, target condition |
| Entry and exit | Authored opening or actual adopted predecessor; location/presence, look, prop, knowledge, relationship and performed state |
| Tactic and process | Stimulus→strategy→onset/action/contact→feedback→hold/release; concurrent listeners/background behavior |
| Attention / visibility | What must be seen/heard at each phase; viewpoint/scale/depth, occlusion/focus, reveal permission |
| Camera / cut | Motivated geometry/path/pace/landing; cut trigger and sound/action crossing it |
| Art / sound / effects | Color/material/light or effect job; actual words/voice/cues, atmosphere, music/quiet and any post prerequisites |
| Carrier / evidence | Real asset/input/prompt/post duty and current version; timing, uncertainty and observable failure |

Simple shots can use short prose. Expand complexity only where it removes ambiguity. Textual world/voice/asset names must resolve to actual direction and bindings; the model cannot read project notes. Masters own identity/current look and usable space; scene/shot references own local state, contact and composition.

## Expand the performed process

For important contact, specify body support, approach/grip, object holder/state, weight/consequence and release as relevant. For acting, specify the stimulus, tactic, visible/audible change and listener response. For reveals, control what is withheld, first readable moment, affected person and aftermath. Avoid hand gestures or gasps added to every line.

Direct camera/attention through real geometry and a motivated change; fixed cameras can shift attention through blocking, focus and sound. Stylized physics/effects need coherent local rules and visible consequences. Keep source observations distinct from selected target lens/lighting settings. If post owns an effect, define generated clean view, tracking/occlusion and timing prerequisites; post cannot invent missing contact, acting or room geography.

## Rehearse the ear and the cut with the scene

[Scene design](scene-design.md#rehearse-only-unresolved-timing-and-readability) owns adaptive rehearsal/animatic, spatial scale and state. [Sound direction](sound-direction.md) owns hearing perspective, voice tactics, music and silence. Reuse accepted material for an already settled uncertainty; do not launch separate auditions, look tests and continuity trials where one representative passage can resolve the actual questions.

Bind cuts/cues to meaningful words, actions and discoveries, reserving listener and aftermath time. Separate source, authored scene, block-local request and actual adopted seconds. Requested handles are production margin, not film duration. The final line's consequence, title passage or quiet can be the unit's ending, not its last syllable.

Use the [model/post/hybrid effect card](../production/post-design.md) before committing generation.
An artistic title needs accepted glyph/material/motion and a working render path; complex
compositing needs actual plates, masks/tracking and light/occlusion. Proof the mechanism with
proxy media where possible, then accept its integration on adopted footage.

## Allocate references by shot duty, then compile H3

Select actual identity/look, geography/state, acting/contact, composition/light, camera/rhythm and voice controls needed by the current unit within verified app limits. Check contradictory references, leakage and premature reveal. A still cannot supply temporal acting; a neutral portrait does not establish integrated scene photography.

Write the official [H3 mode and fields](../playbooks/craft/h3-prompts.md); that page owns exact syntax and section duties. The main description unfolds the resolved picture/sound timeline, with supported inputs and retention/reuse scopes. Keep planning metadata outside model text. If duration/text capacity is insufficient, remove redundancy or regroup blocks while preserving the complete unit; do not truncate consequences.

Apply [reference-first compilation](../playbooks/craft/h3-dense-direction.md#参考负责静态事实正文负责差异与过程): references supply their verified static facts; text supplies missing/changed facts and the full temporal process. Do not re-copy asset descriptions into each section or shot. Complete director/audience prose remains in STORYBOARD; its enacted conditions remain individually traceable across real carriers.

Reverse-read the **final serialized request plus bound inputs**: which sequence would these alone cause, which obligations remain only in planning notes, what new unsupported behavior would they introduce? Compare to the source unit and target conditions; then use the single [combined gate](../production/production-gate.md).

## Adopt reality and reopen affected work

Choose real usable intervals; retain the unit's setup/change/reaction/aftermath, then update descendants and cue timing from adopted boundary states. A failed tail cannot become a continuation reference. Grouped/split/pickup coverage must allocate all obligations across the actual selected cut.

Repairs record defect→changed owner→preserved experience→dramatic cost→actual comparison. An insert that fixes writing can still consume a necessary listener reaction. [Scene acceptance](../production/scene-acceptance.md) closes single/boundary/scene/export conditions against real playback; completeness of prompts or tiny repair passes cannot settle the whole unit.

## Compact directing card example

```text
Unit: anticipation of a valuable gift → ordinary key reveal → laughter → intimacy.
Setup: her left hand supports the box; the lid still hides its content; he waits across the table.
Process: she tests his expression, touches the rim, opens the lid toward herself while he stops
stirring; keep contents hidden until the planned insert. Paper friction and quiet support waiting.
Reveal: the key first becomes fully readable in the insert; a small metal sound belongs to it.
Response: return to her confusion changing to a laugh; his relieved response remains visible.
End: she closes her right hand around the key, still supports the box with her left, looks at him.
Sound/score: no dialogue here; choose score entry only if it helps the late intimacy, not before
the reveal. Post follows adopted cue time; final viewing must retain the laugh and resulting look.
```

This is planning prose to compile, not another H3 prompt format or a plot to copy into unrelated work.
