# Voice and performance in short drama

Read when organizing dialogue, listener coverage, off-screen speech and recurring voice.
First establish the [scene sound intention](../../creation/sound-direction.md): who the audience
hears with, information/reveal timing and the emotional change, then choose the sound path. [Voice direction](voice-direction.md) owns casting; character voice decisions and accepted samples belong in CHARACTERS/ASSETS.

## Give a passage the performance it needs

A Script Segment is a performable passage. Visible dialogue may include several turns, listeners and cuts; a wordless reaction has real performed boundaries. The generated/adopted media supplies actual duration, and alignment supplies words. Do not create a separate clock merely because another character enters frame. [Timeline](../../production/timeline.md) owns assembly.

## Keep speaker and picture separate

A character's line can continue over the listener, evidence insert or relevant scene detail while the speaking performance remains the sound source. A character's inner voice may instead be independent speech. Seeing a face during a line does not establish that its lips should move. Record who speaks, where they are, who hears, visible mouth behavior and the picture's editorial purpose.

Use [Performance](../../production/performance.md) for existing Timeline footage, [Media](../../production/media-presentation.md) for independently placed views and [Sound](../../production/sound.md) for the selected audio. A visual crossfade does not require overlapping dialogue. Explicit interruptions/overlap/pauses follow the performed scene, not a default transition template.

## Choose the sound path before generation

Use [the scene sound/performance path](../../production/shot-blocks.md#choose-the-sound-and-performance-path). An accepted exact dialogue track drives timing only when the live app exposes that control. Otherwise a voice reference is guidance and the H3 result must be inspected for actual words, voice, mouth and reactions. Scratch speech sizes an animatic; it is not automatically the final track.

## Carry one accepted voice into varied acting

Every speaking character has a distinguishable vocal profile and a clean representative sample when needed: language/accuracy, age impression, pitch/timbre, breath/grain, baseline rhythm, relevant emotion and pause behavior. Reuse the same accepted reference/ID through that person's scenes within verified app limits. A short clear sample excludes other speakers, music, heavy echo and clipping; exact length/count follows the route.

Character identity does not freeze emotional delivery. Whispering, anger, teasing and a strained breath can retain one person while serving different tactics. Record important dialect, pronunciation and stress in the literary script/compiled spoken text, without inventing unsupported model controls. [Script/time](../../creation/script-and-time.md) owns display/pronunciation and real alignment.

## Listen through the adopted scene

Check voice continuity, words, turn order, mouth behavior, vocal distance, reaction holds and acoustic space across actual edit intervals. Music and physical sound follow the scene's cue plan. A correct voice sample or readable transcript cannot settle acting or final mixing. [Sound/mix](sound-mix.md) and [scene acceptance](../../production/scene-acceptance.md) close those checks.
