# Narrative inserts and supporting coverage

Use an insert, reaction, memory, environment detail or off-screen clue for its dramatic job. A close view of evidence can change the viewer's knowledge; a listener can make the previous line funny; a distant sound paired with an empty doorway can increase anticipation. Do not fill a silent moment with arbitrary attractive imagery.

Record the triggering action/line, viewer question, what becomes readable, start/end visibility and the next view's relation in the written storyboard. Use native coverage when a small wide-shot crop loses the telling detail. An insert must preserve its character's hand/prop state and reveal permission.

Still or moving material depends on what unfolds: a letter detail may be static, opening its fold or a trembling grip requires motion. Keep accepted source/adopted intervals and actual sound responsibilities explicit. Covering a speaker does not create a new voice source; [voice/performance](voice-and-performance.md) and [sound](../../production/sound.md) retain the underlying speech relationship.

Generate within the same world, current look and [palette](../../creation/style-lock.md), using [image](image-direction.md)/[video direction](video-direction.md). Present with [Media](../../production/media-presentation.md) or current Timeline [Performance](../../production/performance.md) according to the actual timing owner. Recheck the scene after insertion so setup, reaction and emotional hold survive.
