# Short-drama graphic motion and designed effects

Before H3 submission use [post design](../../production/post-design.md) to allocate model/post/hybrid
duties and real prerequisites. For artistic titles, masks/tracking or unverified effects, a
representative rendered proof and actual implementation are required for the declared path;
a prose plan or a generic fade does not establish that mechanism.

Start from a dramatic purpose: intensify a reaction, make a reveal legible, connect world layers or give a title its intended emotional force. Preserve the scene's human/causal spine. World effects may be stylized while material/light still integrate convincingly; visual realism does not require removing deliberate exaggeration.

Define initial state, trigger, change/peak, readable hold, release and end state, linked to the actual adopted action/word/cue. Assign generation versus post duties: generated occlusion/contact/camera action must exist; post owns the planned join/overlay/tracking when chosen. A named effect without timed prerequisites is not executable.

Use visual-manual color/shape/material rules and the actual source's register. If a graphic tracks a face or prop, specify target, coordinate mapping, visibility/occlusion and failure conditions. [Component visuals](../../production/component-visuals.md) and [timing](../../production/timing.md) own frame-driven implementation; [caption tracking](caption-tracking.md) applies only to that requested behavior.

Check the effect with complete motion/sound and neighboring cuts; preserve reading, reaction and emotional residue. Artifact-free rendering is a technical check, while whether the effect lands remains a viewing judgment.
