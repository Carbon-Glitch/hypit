# Directing short-drama images

Read before any asset or shot-image prompt. Use [documented direction](../../creation/drama-preproduction.md), [asset duties](../../creation/asset-plan.md) and [reference sheets](../../creation/drama-reference-sheets.md). For photographed drama also read [cinematic look](../../creation/cinematic-look.md); stylized drama uses its accepted visual manual.

## Two ideas behind every sentence

Choose concrete anchors that change this picture: face/body/wardrobe, the social character of the place, material/light, camera distance and visible relationships. A full asset brief is appropriate when identity or geometry is being established; a derived shot prompt spends detail on the new composition and state.

## Cast and frame for the assigned duty

The neutral character sheet owns identity and current look; a performance reference owns a particular expression, posture or contact; a first frame owns one frozen starting state. Keep those permissions separate. The four-panel character master has front/profile head-and-half-body and front/back full-body views. A recurring scene starts with one 3/4 overview; add consumed camera/state views or an optional multiview sheet. Inspect shared geography rather than assuming it from a grid. Final single-shot frames are clean single images derived from accepted masters, not boards showing four people or nine simultaneous pictures.

Direct face angle, body arrangement, foreground/midground/depth, scale and attention as one physical view. A reaction close-up needs readable eyes/mouth at intended output size. A two-person contact needs both bodies, support and hands to agree.

## Establish the place with a few details

A classroom door, a window light source and occupied desk rows can explain the encounter and survive reverse coverage. Connect entrances, fixed objects, scale and traversable space; each camera sees its plausible side of that same place. Materials and colors should live on specific objects, not wash every surface with the same hue. Apply the accepted [global/scene palette](../../creation/style-lock.md) and actual motivated lighting.

## Let references supply the facts they own

Specify each actual input's identity/look, geometry, color, state or composition duty. Add an exclusion only for an actual conflicting detail in that input. Read [reference dependencies](generated-dependencies.md) and [control scopes](../../production/reference-scopes.md). A source reaction still may own expression while the new cast owns face; an old costume reference can own identity only when a new accepted look supplies clothing.

The chosen image Surface/Kit documentation owns exact reference inputs, aspect/resolution and syntax.

## Judge reuse and shot reality separately

Check every sheet panel and the whole master for identity, front/back construction, mirrored details and connected space. A clean identity sheet can be accepted while the location-lit shot still fails exposure, focus, depth or material integration. Inspect the actual single frame before it becomes a video input; the first frame depicts the authored opening state. Save prompt, actual inputs, version, acceptance evidence and consuming shots in ASSETS.
