# Post-finishing and delivery QC

Use after candidate selection and during scene assembly. The Brief and actual destination determine
format and quality requirements. Do not turn one platform's specification or one film's look into a
universal delivery rule. Preserve originals and finish from the adopted intervals.

Read [dramatic craft](../creation/dramatic-craft.md#finish-defects-without-spending-the-scenes-meaning)
and [artistic review](short-drama-production.md) when repairs or technical finishing have begun to dictate
the scene. Maintain setup/payoff/reaction and the changing attention/relationship through each
repair. Provisional sample export and full artistic acceptance are separate outcomes.
Carry the [director blueprint](../creation/director-blueprint.md)'s adopted action/word/cue anchors
into the edit rather than retaining planned block seconds. Use [acceptance evidence](acceptance-evidence.md)
to close every essential experience and applicable title/sound condition against the actual export;
an available asset, mixing instruction or render success is not proof of realization.

The [post-design/effect cards](post-design.md) are resolved during storyboard/preproduction.
They name model/post/hybrid ownership, required generation plates/handles, implementation,
representative proof and final integration. Confirm selected source ranges/picture version,
handoff/conform effect and sound work, then finish and review the actual encoded export.

## Finish according to the defects present

Conform [titles](title-design.md#实际剪辑与出口验收) from their dramatic event anchors, not from the
number of generated blocks before them. Inspect first visibility, full readability, read-end and exit
on the actual export, plus the complete surrounding beats and sound. Reuse accepted lettering/layout;
do not substitute an available font or freeze footage simply to fill an animation template.

Respect [strict conservation](../creation/content-conservation.md) during every repair and trim.
Remove only non-contributing technical handles/bad frames; if a cut also removes a protected line,
setup, listener reaction, director/audience purpose or camera/sound expression, repair the coverage
instead of labeling the loss "tighter editing". Acceptance v2 closes each obligation and target
inventory against the actual export, including heard dialogue independent of corrected captions.

First revisit the [editorial/attention plan](../creation/attention-and-rhythm.md): usable generated
seconds are raw coverage, not the final pace. Recover required setup/payoff/reaction, choose J/L or
reaction coverage when motivated, and inspect accumulated same-energy exchanges, title holds and
expository passages in the whole cut. Fast playback, extra music/effects, a global 1.2x speed preset or a new insert do not
automatically repair an earlier adaptation or dialogue failure. Use local speed only when its source interval, reason, factor and preserved experience are recorded.

Make sound/graphics/effects concrete at actual adopted cue points, with completed proofs and final
integration evidence. If the editable timeline uses a normalized replacement mix, mute the original
clip audio and verify the preview matches export; preserve originals and declare whether separate
dialogue/music/effect stems really exist. Picture timing changes invalidate total-mix/subtitle sync.
Check actual spoken words against captions: corrected target text cannot conceal wrong generated
speech. Normal-speed complete export viewing/listening still determines audiovisual acceptance.

A useful starting order is temporal repair (including only evidence-backed local retiming), necessary resizing/upscaling, shot balancing/matching,
then intentional final texture and export. After any retime, reconform subtitles, titles, sound cues, bridges and downstream shot boundaries before the next acceptance pass. Inspect a representative passage before batch processing.
The actual dependency order depends on the tool and source; no operation is mandatory when its
problem is absent, and color-space conversions may need to precede particular processing tools.

For a requested professional photographed look, use
[cinematic quality conditions](../creation/cinematic-look.md) and inspect original H3 output against
the actual processed version. Distinguish generated defects from damage introduced by crop, scale,
matte, grading, retiming or compression. Preserve native and processed variants with their adopted
intervals; a uniform final filter cannot restore all of those losses.

## Compositing and native detail

When replacing a backdrop or joining visual layers, match perspective/scale and lens/focus behavior,
subject/background exposure and light color/direction, contact shadows/reflections and foreground
occlusion. Inspect hair, semi-transparent fabric, motion edges and any spill/fringe left by the old
background. Respect the actual tool's alpha interpretation; a hard cutout or conflicting sharpness
can reveal the composite even when geography is correct. Check transitions and movement, not only
one still. Preserve deliberate stylized effects and source-observed acting.

Use a generated integrated shot frame or corrected local layer when matching cannot rescue the
composite. A simpler shot may reduce risk while retaining required information; it cannot silently
replace a critical physical interaction or flatten source expression. A note claiming matched is
not evidence of the result.

For digital reframing, record source dimensions, crop dimensions, scale/output dimensions and the
reason for use. Judge face/hair/fabric detail and focus at final size, plus their behavior in motion.
A close-up enlarged from a small wide-shot patch can meet pixel dimensions yet miss the photographic
target. Prefer planned native close-up/insert/reverse coverage where the crop does not survive;
there is no universal enlargement cutoff independent of source quality and Brief. Upscaling may
improve presentation but is not a new camera view or proof of recovered native detail.

## Color and exposure matching

Use the current VISUAL global/scene card and accepted integrated hero frames as comparison
targets. Color-table values describe assigned roles, not uniform pixel tolerances across
differently lit objects. Verify costume/prop semantic colors and deliberate local variation
through the actual adopted export, preserving the intended source/target experience.

Establish actual source color interpretation and target display/delivery intent: known primaries,
transfer/range and relevant tool conversions. Unknown tags remain unknown until assessed; adding a
label alone does not perform a color conversion. Do not treat an SDR generated file as RAW/log or
apply a transform intended for another capture pipeline just because it is a cinematic LUT.

[ACES](https://docs.acescentral.com/system-components/output-transforms/) is a color-management
option requiring appropriate input/output transforms, not a required
film effect or a substitute for balancing. A print-emulation LUT such as Kodak 2383 is a creative
choice with a defined expected input; verify that interpretation and the actual processed result.
Neither a LUT nor lifted blacks recover already clipped highlights/shadows or correct mismatched
light direction. Preserve source/accepted look intentions instead of forcing every scene darker.

Balance the shot's exposure/white balance and preserve the intended highlight/shadow detail; then
match same-scene shots against comparable subject or neutral surfaces, allowing motivated light
changes. Use appropriate scopes where available, but do not force every complexion to a single
saturation/brightness or use whole-frame averages across different compositions as a quality verdict.
Only after matching choose the creative look/texture. Check that saturation, contrast, sharpening
and any denoising have not made skin waxy, foliage fluorescent, blacks unreadable or edges brittle.

Optical softness/selective focus differs from indiscriminate blur or lost resolution. Grain, bloom,
flare and blur must suit this project's look and should not hide failed photographic integration.
Inspect the actual encoded export in the intended viewer: planned grade values, target bitrate,
resolution or codec grain metadata do not establish the visible result. Avoid unnecessary repeated
lossy encoding; use suitable intermediates where processing requires them and compare final detail.

- Separate exposure flicker from changing generated geometry/texture and from intentional lighting.
  Pick the repair that addresses that defect. Interpolation or smoothing can create ghosting at
  contacts and occlusions; recheck those regions and performance timing. Do not prescribe model
  parameters the RunningHub app does not expose.
- Upscaling changes dimensions and may reconstruct detail; it cannot certify missing native detail.
  Distinguish native resolution from delivery resolution. Inspect faces, fabric and moving edges
  at the target size. If the Brief requires native detail unavailable from the permitted H3 route,
  disclose the gap rather than switching models or labeling an upscale native 4K.
- Balance exposure/white balance and match comparable surfaces to the scene's accepted hero view.
  Preserve intentional light changes and skin variation. Choose grading/LUT strength from the image
  and color pipeline; there is no universal percentage ceiling or required LUT.
- Grain and blur are optional aesthetics. If used, judge at delivery size and avoid erasing fine
  detail or compounding existing noise. Do not add them simply because the material was generated.

## State continuity through the edit

Use [spatial continuity](spatial-continuity.md) on actual adopted pairs and returning props. In existing anchors, mark indispensable observed transitions as kind=state_change, required=true with actual source start/end. retime_finish restores omitted parts before remapping cues, so trimming a lead-in cannot erase a handoff, impact or aftermath. Re-extract changed adopted result frames and refresh only affected successor state. Do not horizontally flip a whole shot to repair one cup/hand.

## Speech preservation before cuts and at export

Use [actual dialogue protection](dialogue-preservation.md) at the real edit boundary. retime_finish protects source speech and compares its rendered audio; custom runners must use the same checks. A caption interval must be fully retained and timed from observed speech, never accepted because a fragment overlaps it. The final encoded file needs blind transcription compared offline with target lines. 当前Agent success, complete subtitles and equal picture/audio lengths cannot clear missing words or unresolved recognition. Repair affected ranges and reconform cues before acceptance.

## Sound finishing

Use [sound direction](../creation/sound-direction.md#粗剪后的导演声音定案与执行) for the rough-cut
spotting/disposition, then finish its selected native H3 sound and added layers. Inspect the
actual native mix before adding score; a user/basic listener's time-addressed observation can
establish its state. Professional source-analysis listening is optional in deferred mode,
while actual target listening and post/export checks remain applicable.

Close the [sound-directing intent](../creation/sound-direction.md), including hearing perspective,
expectation/reveal order, vocal tactics, listener response, cue turn and emotional aftermath.
Choose final music by scene function; do not automatically spread one loop across every
scene or remove a hold to fit the bed. Inspect actual native H3 sound before deciding which
layers to retain, repair or add, without claiming stems that were never returned.

For cinematic sound, check actual delivery, timing and acoustic relationship as well as technical
levels: word clarity/prosody, speaker identity, listener pauses, voice distance/room perspective,
contact and off-screen cue synchronization, continuing ambience, effect transients, meaningful
silence and score focus. Independent stems must not duplicate or contradict H3's baked-in audio.
Use supported voice/sound inputs and authorized preparation services; do not imply an unavailable
audio reference or external generative lip-sync route. A loudness pass or ASR transcript cannot
verify these qualities. If normal-speed listening is unavailable, keep the auditory gate unverified.

Close the [scene cue plan](../playbooks/craft/sound-mix.md#plan-drama-sound-before-generation) against
the adopted edit/export: required cues have actual audible intervals, intended perspective and
foreground/background placement. Missing or doubled contact sounds, an erased quiet beat or changed
music motif are repair findings even when speech and loudness pass. Keep the plan and its realized
timing together in existing records after trims, retiming or candidate replacement.

For recreation, carry source music/effect motifs, vocal texture, accents and meaningful silence
through the accepted change scope. Inspect whether score and atmosphere actually contribute the
same rhythm and response; continuous room tone alone does not reproduce a scene's sound design.
Retain or recreate cues through permitted audio work and actual post instructions, with their source
detail IDs, planned timing and final listening evidence. Do not invent access to music services or
reuse permissions; already settled accounts/budgets/rights remain authoritative.

Inspect actual H3 audio before adding score or ambience. A no-music prompt does not prove a clean
stem was returned. Preserve useful original speech and contact sounds, avoid doubled music, and
repair acoustic seams without cutting syllables or changing speaker attribution.

Choose score placement, level automation/ducking, compression and limiting to serve intelligibility
and the intended dynamics; none is universally mandatory or forbidden. Use the actual destination's
loudness, true-peak, sample-rate and channel requirements. If they are unspecified, document a
proposed delivery preset rather than claiming a global platform standard. Measure the final encoded
file, listen at normal level and check sync after any retiming or audio replacement. Refer to
[sound and mix](../playbooks/craft/sound-mix.md) for composition choices.

## Delivery and viewing

Before choosing cuts, log each candidate's actually usable source ranges and excluded ranges with
observable reasons. The edit selects within those ranges and records separate in-point/out-point
reasons. A pickup or alternate does not replace its master by filename order; follow
[production calibration](production-calibration.md)'s detail allocation and coverage proof.

Organize the edit by setup, action/payoff and reaction. Do not automatically remove pauses, holds,
expression peaks or listener close-ups as dead time: compare their source function and expressive
effect first. The original shot order need not be copied mechanically, but changed coverage must
retain its readable detail and rhythm. Recheck after trims, retiming and reframing.

Give each retained designed visual effect a post owner, source evidence, prerequisites, tracking or
mask needs, timing/intensity and final rendered evidence. Stylized color, facial emphasis, impact
frames and similar devices are part of fidelity when they carry the source response; do not reduce
them to subtle realism unless that change is within scope. A textual post plan is not proof the
effect was executed. Compare the final look/space/sound signature with representative source passages.

Verify actual dimensions, aspect, frame rate, codec, bitrate/quality, audio, subtitles and required
labels against the Brief and verified destination requirements. Neither 9:16 nor >=25 fps is a
universal requirement. Reframing another ratio must preserve the revealing prop, listener and action,
not just center the largest face. Do not invent a compliance requirement from an unverified report.

Watch the assembled scene/episode at normal speed with sound, then inspect flagged frames and
transitions. Check identity/look, performance causality, background presence, contact/hand geometry,
texture shimmer, frame drops/interpolation, broken text and grading seams. Review on the intended
viewing surface, including a phone when that is the audience's primary device. Dense frame checks
support playback; they cannot replace it.

Track each defect by adopted file/version, interval, cause, repair and recheck result in the existing
edit/acceptance records. Repeated defects trigger investigation of their common upstream cause,
not automatic blame based on a fixed occurrence count. Preserve rejected candidates for traceability.

Use the accepted full target storyboard (and an already-used literary body when applicable) for subtitle words and pronunciation/display choices; ASR
locates speech and detects discrepancies but does not silently rewrite adopted text. If actual speech
differs, resolve whether to repair media or revise the owner before final subtitles. Re-align after
candidate replacement and derive cumulative caption positions from measured edited intervals, not
planned generation lengths. Check the final encoded captions and meaningful picture text.
Close [story transfer](story-transfer.md) against the final export: every retained critical event
must be observable, not merely documented in a prompt.

For an artistic title, inspect actual accepted glyph geometry, material/depth, contour/mask
reveal, local light or other chosen effect, readable hold/exit and sound, not only a text layer
or default opacity animation. Use a working code/Track/HTML/SVG/compositing project; retain
alpha interpretation and inspect it against the adopted background.

Inspect applicable title passages as their own sound/picture handoff: intended trigger, image,
type hierarchy/material, appearance/reading/exit and the neighboring performance. Verify glyphs
and animation in the encoded output. A subtitle preset, corner placement or generic fade is not
automatically an adequate replacement for a designed source title. Do not add a title when the
brief omits one. Correct typography and technical export do not establish the scene's attraction.

Use the same whole-output playback to [check the commissioned work](scene-acceptance.md#check-the-whole-commissioned-work),
including selected improvements and cumulative effects of repairs. For requested localization
or editable audio handoff, retain available separate dialogue/music/effects and textless elements
when needed; declare what is actually separable. A mixed H3 original is not a set of clean stems,
and derived separation remains a processed candidate requiring listening. Channel/codec/package
choices follow this commission and actual tool/destination, not another studio's mandatory formats.

## Commissioned release and feedback

Deliver editable project/Source/Run, accepted master sheets, actual palette images, cue/grade
versions, adoption records and non-secret job/prompt/input/cost history with the commissioned
outputs. If publication is part of the task, verify current destination-specific requirements
and use the established publication authority; do not import old manual prices or legal claims.
制作观察或用户修改意见按实际文件/区间保存，修正对应责任层并复看。此次流程不要求发布数据、
跳出/完播分析或观众反馈定位；基本声音/画面接受和已提供的修改意见仍照常处理。

## Autonomous finishing is part of the commission

[Autonomous delivery](autonomous-delivery.md) requires an actual rhythm edit, concrete native/post sound treatment, graphic/effect integration and encoded-output checks before handing over a full film. Lack of normal-speed perception leaves artistic results unverified; it does not stop available editing/mixing/rendering or require another authorization. Track actual cut/word/action/reaction anchors and before/after duration, not just the concatenation of full model clips. Use [inspect_finishing.py](../../scripts/inspect_finishing.py) for decoder/stream, silence, black/freeze and loudness/peak diagnosis; give actual transcript timestamps only when available. Measurement candidates are not auto-trims, and no score certifies acting or emotion. Repair identified issues within authorized scope, export again, and disclose precisely what remains. A rendered MP4 plus an unexecuted finishing plan does not satisfy the commission.
