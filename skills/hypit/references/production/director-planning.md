# Director planning: coverage, knowledge and frozen boundaries

Use for reference-led or original drama when converting accepted scene decisions into shots.
Existing Treatment and the shot table own these decisions; the source detail catalogue remains
independent evidence. Ordinary scenes need no new comparison document or fixed shot quota.
Use [director execution contracts](director-contract.md) for relevant control/phase/layer decisions.
Check the chosen plan against source obligations in the normal writing/adoption pass; do not
create another independent review, coverage report or fixed-count audition.

Use the [current-Agent writing workflow](../creation/document-writing.md) for creative shot allocation,
reveal/coverage choices and new directing prose. GPT prepares actual capabilities, source evidence
and timing observations, then compiles the accepted plan without inventing missing shots or actions.

## Compare genuinely different execution where it matters

For a high-value reveal, relationship turn, difficult interaction or ending, compare plausible
coverage approaches when the choice changes readability, timing, performance ownership or H3 fit.
Vary a real directing relationship, not merely the name of a shot size: who owns attention, when
proof becomes readable, whether a listener or the action leads, or whether sound precedes confirmation.

| Approach | Audience alignment and knowledge timing | Strongest image/performance | Coverage losses | Fit within the actual RunningHub H3 inputs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Candidate | Who the viewer follows and when the fact lands | What must remain readable | Reaction, geography, contact, atmosphere or information risk | Reference slots, duration, action/speech load |

For experience-led recreation, candidates preserve essential experience, causal/relationship
obligations and chosen information dependencies through their target mechanism; source forms
bind only where explicitly retained. Reorder or redesign coverage with a recorded gain/loss
and viewing test. Model convenience is not experience equivalence. Reuse an already settled approach. Choose routine implementation
within the accepted direction; bring a concrete material creative trade-off to the user only when
not already resolved by the Brief. Comparison does not require paid samples or another agent.

## Specify information permission at its carrier

For each consequential fact, record in its shot row: source detail; who already knows it; what the
audience can see/hear now; what remains withheld; the visible/audible trigger releasing it; and how
framing, occlusion, focus, sound or text delivers/protects it. Voice and visual identity can reveal
at different times. Inspect both accidental early disclosure and intended evidence hidden by a
beautiful composition. Never add a reveal solely because the reference image happens to contain it.

Visible-surface text has an explicit treatment: exact readable wording, symbolic/unreadable, blank,
or post overlay. Preserve the literal target wording at its owning Script/asset record. A generic
no-text/no-logo clause cannot erase an intended proof item or needed picture text. Conversely, a
reference's unrelated signs or subtitles must not become unintended story evidence.

## Give each shot a dominant job and an executable interval

For cinematic work, use [photographic look](../creation/cinematic-look.md) to choose view/perspective,
focus and lighting for the shot's attention job. Close-ups/inserts/reverse views should be designed
as genuine coverage using the shared scene state, not routinely extracted by enlarging a small patch
of a wide output. Record native dimensions, crop/enlargement and final-size quality when a repair
uses digital reframing. A crop preserving the plot still needs photographic acceptance.

Record the source details served, attention/information job, camera/view, entry and exit boundaries,
necessary action order, listener consequences, camera behavior/reason, proposed duration and next-cut
purpose. Concurrent action is legal when it serves that job and stays readable; one job does not
mean one verb. Stillness can be an active choice. Do not invent motion merely to avoid a static frame.

Derive duration from actual or scratch speech, sequential physical phases, reactions and settling.
Mark real overlap rather than pretending prerequisites happen simultaneously. Sum authored edit-shot
seconds and list unresolved durations before preparing a batch. Separately sum generated seconds
and planned adopted seconds: handles, discarded ranges and multiple cuts from one block make them
different quantities. Compare with any Brief duration/tolerance and record the disposition. Do not
uniformly shorten shots, delete payoff or accelerate speech to make arithmetic fit.

For key beats, identify which planned shots/sound/post jointly provide the cause, discovery,
listener and consequence that must survive editing. A generated block can supply several cuts,
and one beat can need several blocks. Reserve handles or alternate/native reaction/insert
coverage only for a specific timing/readability risk; reuse sufficient accepted coverage.
The source edit proves its selected arrangement, not its unseen discarded takes or shoot plan.
Do not add fixed alternate counts or generate every potential angle.

## Freeze one declared boundary

A keyframe projects a shot's accepted start or end; it is not a compressed motion prompt or a new
source of story state. Name that boundary and inspect the resulting prose/image against it:

- Every visible participant's body facing, position, gaze, hands and held props agree with the boundary.
- Framing, depth layers, attention/focus, local light and relevant look are readable at the target size.
- The frame includes only facts already true at that instant and obeys the reveal/text permission.
- It contains one expression state, not an expression arc; ordered action and camera motion belong
  to the H3 description. A frozen movement phase does not mean the story must begin at rest.

After drafting, compare start versus end and remove facts appearing for the first time later. For
an end frame, remove states already consumed by that point. Tail frames are only needed if the verified
app will actually use them. They project the authored end; they cannot certify intermediate contact
or acting. Keep intermediate obligations explicit and inspect the output. Readable text/effects
planned for post still need the suitable surface and visibility in the generated frame.

## Preserve shot identity through revision

Shot IDs name editing/directing decisions, not list positions or provider job IDs. Reordering or
reframing the same responsibility retains the ID. Inserting an independent responsibility creates
one. When a shot is replaced by multiple independent shots, retire its ID and assign new IDs with
predecessor links; merging also creates a new decision with all predecessors. Do not recycle retired
IDs for unrelated shots or renumber every later shot after an insertion.

Update affected source-detail allocation, keyframes, H3 blocks, neighbor boundaries, duration totals
and acceptance records together. Changes that do not affect other shots do not invalidate them.
[Story transfer](story-transfer.md) owns required detail coverage; [reference scopes](reference-scopes.md)
owns what each media input is allowed to determine.
