# Story transfer: preserve performed details through the adopted film

Use for reference-led recreation/localization. Apply the
[fidelity contract](../creation/fidelity-contract.md) before drafting. SOURCE_SHOTS/SOURCE_CONTEXT own
observed details; Analysis owns interpretation; Brief/Treatment own the target change scope; the shot
and edit records own implementation. Compare every stage with original evidence, not only with the
previous summary. Original work follows its own brief; recreation defaults to experience fidelity with free form
and purposeful added value. Form preservation is conditional on explicit literal requirements.

## Detail-based handoff

Also carry the [source experience index](../creation/reference-events.md#cross-shot-experience-units):
EX ID links an ordered setup/expectation/change/reaction/residue and its dependency to original
detail IDs, target mechanism and acceptance condition. One EX unit may span several execution
blocks; do not rename v2 block IDs or build another registry. Detail declarations passing in
different discarded candidates cannot prove the unit survives the adopted sequence.

| Source detail / evidence | Function, observable form, expressive effect | Target decision and observable condition | Shot/block and execution | Actual output evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stable detail ID, source time and clip/frame/word evidence | What it does, how it is shown/heard, why its register matters | Preserve/non-weaker replace; additions separate, no omission | Camera/coverage, actual request passage or bound input, post prerequisite and cue | Candidate version, adopted interval and observed status |

A plot event can contain several actions, performances and reactions. Give those details distinct
IDs when they need different framing, phases, inputs or acceptance conditions. A quote about
background movement cannot cover an unrelated foreground interaction. A correct dialogue line cannot
cover a lost listener close-up. Maintain setup/action/payoff/reaction and their order/overlap.

Original-only observations must precede target jobs. The source catalogue cannot be generated from
the same adapted manifest used to produce the mappings. Freeze its reviewed export and explicitly
reopen it for real corrections. Do not delete source detail IDs, lower their importance or weaken
expected target cues merely to make a retry pass.

First compare the catalogue with the whole scoped source using
[dramatic craft](../creation/dramatic-craft.md#establish-the-attraction-before-translating-the-plot).
Allocated entries can all pass while an unlisted character exchange, title or sound device is lost.
Audit meaningful interval gaps and proposed omissions before freezing; retain the attraction and
emotional progression in target decisions, not only their plot-equivalent information.

Link allocations to the [whole-work/serial transfer](../creation/source-deconstruction.md#从解构承接到制作和成片)
as well as EX units. A new world/language/shot order must make the chosen causality, cultural
tactic, information sequence and meaningful grammar evolution credible in the target. Source
improvement candidates stay hypotheses until selected in Treatment and tested in adopted media;
they do not become new source details or justify silent losses. Keep the existing export schemas.

## Review changes on three axes

For every adapted detail, compare story function, distinctive form and expressive effect. Ordinary
language/casting/surface changes can stay within the accepted Brief; replacing a distinctive physical
interaction, lowering expression intensity, changing genre or deleting revealing coverage does not
become authorized because it serves the same plot purpose. Record the before/after decision and its
basis. Follow the fidelity contract when materially different experience choices remain unresolved.
For default experience-led recreation, form can change under that contract with a testable replacement;
preserve the essential feeling and consequence individually. The three-axis comparison explains
the mechanism change, not a requirement to duplicate every gesture or prop. Use the
[director blueprint](../creation/director-blueprint.md) to compile target conditions, cue/cut
relationships and actual carriers. Gains elsewhere never erase an essential loss.

Static images can support identity, expression peaks, positions or atmosphere. Temporal unfolding
needs explicit phase/timing direction or a viable temporal input. Scene-continuity references do not
necessarily carry acting. Post can supply a designed blush, accent sound or digital effect, but its
generated prerequisites, tracking/visibility and timing must exist and be assigned too.

For a pickup or alternate, name the master and allocate every original obligation to that master,
the pickup, another accepted output or an unresolved gap. Reduced prompt scope does not reduce the
preservation contract. Replacing a master requires coverage proof across the actual adopted cut.
Shot splits/merges keep explicit predecessor/successor links as described in
[director planning](director-planning.md).

## Four clocks and performance read-through

Separate source time, planned target scene time, H3 block-local time and actual adopted in/out time.
Align by detail/event IDs and dramatic triggers. Read the target-language scene with intended pace,
stress, interruptions and physical action; check that it retains relationship, joke and atmosphere.
Reserve time for reaction and settling as well as syllables. Do not speed dialogue or discard reaction
holds to fit a request. When a block is overloaded, split at a motivated boundary while retaining the
source's coverage function and register. Actual output updates timing, not the frozen source facts.

## Compile into the official H3 structure

Use the chosen mode's guide and the verified RunningHub app inputs. Do not transplant another
model's prompt syntax. For Ref2VA:

| Section | Required responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| subject_definitions | Subjects/speakers and real input/region bindings; current look and other scopes by concise reference, not repeated appearance inventories |
| summary | The actual supported task and this block's action/performance purpose |
| retention_analysis | What supplied references contribute/change; this does not replace detail-level source comparison |
| detailed_description | Style; per-shot composition and readable coverage; exact participants and simultaneous actions; action phases; stimulus-linked expression/voice; camera/focus; dialogue, synchronized sound; entry/exit state |
| overall_soundscape | Continuing acoustic space and texture, with timed physical/design sounds assigned to their events above |
| non_diegetic_music | Deliberate score or supported absence; scene-level ownership prevents arbitrary block-to-block BGM |

Follow [reference-first compilation](../playbooks/craft/h3-dense-direction.md#参考负责静态事实正文负责差异与过程).
Stable facts may be shared inside one request. Every independent request carries its applicable
identity/look, light/atmosphere through actual bound references or necessary text, plus its constraints.
Do not re-describe static facts already supplied by the references. Expand named rules and preserve required media
bindings. Preserve original observed intensity, stylization and contrast; photorealistic casting does
not imply restrained acting. Check negative instructions against the detail catalogue: no smile,
no exaggerated effect, no close-up, minimal movement or similar wording can contradict the source.

The official guide's normal 350–500 English words for detailed_description is a writing aid, not a
coverage threshold. Describe relevant detail without padding. Under a real text/input limit, trim
redundancy or restructure the block; do not silently truncate action, response or atmosphere. Keep
all tracing metadata beside the request, outside the official prompt fields.

## Compare the actual request before submission

During normal writing/adoption, compare selected source/EX obligations against the current target
rows and final H3/input/post carriers. Keep complete action, listener, reveal and sound duties;
prompt keywords or a filled ID cannot establish equivalence. Correct gaps at their existing owner.
The [submission check](production-gate.md) validates actual transport bytes and input bindings, not
an allocation/content-review package. It cannot certify source understanding or artistic quality.
After generation use [actual viewing evidence](acceptance-evidence.md) to judge adopted outcomes.

## Close the loop in the actual film

Review the target unit as a readable standalone sequence before aligned source comparison.
Check setup and delayed payoff/motif links, when each character/viewer learns the information,
the listener's consequence and final residue. A beautiful shot, detail-ID coverage or approved
replacement rationale is insufficient if the actual cut drops an essential phase or dependency.

Acceptance compares original media and adopted output side by side or in aligned playback, at the
same meaningful phase. Record each detail as matched, weakened, missing, intentionally changed or
unverified with evidence. A beautiful frame, unchanged face and complete dialogue do not settle the
performance. Inspect the final selected intervals and finished export: a detail present in discarded
footage is absent from the film. Under [strict conservation](../creation/content-conservation.md), no indexed
creative contribution at any importance may remain weakened/missing/unverified in a
completed faithful delivery unless its changed scope is explicitly settled and reported.

For serial work, keep knowledge, relationships, outstanding setups/payoffs and persistent versions
in Treatment. For audience fit, use note-free playback and actual target-language feedback when
available; label predictions as predictions. Publishing tests or contacting viewers needs the user's
authorization. Neither inferred audience response nor a numerical checker score replaces source
comparison.
