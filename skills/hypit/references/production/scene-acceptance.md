# Scene acceptance: judge the adopted cut, then the scene

For drama, a downloaded successful job is a candidate. Acceptance belongs to the actual adopted
media, its usable source interval and its neighbors. [Review](review.md) owns general viewing;
this page owns continuity, performance and repair decisions.

Carry the applicable [artistic conditions](short-drama-production.md) from preparation into actual adopted
media. Judge attraction, recognizable behavior/relationships, expectation and emotional changes,
performed/editorial rhythm, sound/voice and title/graphic passages as well as continuity. A
preparation `ready` is not an output verdict. A speed preset or technically valid retime is not an editorial verdict; inspect the retimed adopted interval with its sound, subtitles, titles and neighbors. Genre and the accepted direction choose the register;
neutral realism or restraint is not a default improvement.

For recreation, use the frozen original detail catalogue and
[fidelity contract](../creation/fidelity-contract.md). Continuity and story coverage are necessary;
the essential experience, register and causal chain must also survive. Literal recreation preserves
required forms; authorized redesign tests its recorded equivalent or stronger target mechanism.
Added polish elsewhere cannot offset a missing essential joke, expectation or consequence. Compare original
and adopted target at the same meaningful phase, using aligned clips rather than arbitrary stills.

Source listening may be [explicitly deferred](source-audio-policy.md) in subtitle/visual-first
adaptation. Compare inspected source facts and declared target sound conditions, not imagined
original tone/score. Keep source-sound fidelity unknown; the new cut still needs actual audio
inspection. User/basic-listener feedback is valid scoped evidence when its real file/interval
and observations are recorded; a professional analysis service is not mandatory.

Before speech acceptance, follow [dialogue preservation](dialogue-preservation.md): check actual source speech retention and the encoded output against target lines. A 当前Agent report with empty conditions or a complete subtitle list is not a dialogue-completeness result; retain and repair missing/uncertain words.

For spatially dependent shots, use [actual continuity observations](spatial-continuity.md): inspect the adopted result and successor entry before recording facts; compare handedness, object ownership/count/location, persistent damage/open/overturned states and camera projection. A successful state checker proves declared carriers/provenance only. Recheck an object when it becomes visible again, not only adjacent face close-ups.

## Three viewing passes

These are distinct judgments/contexts, not three forced replays of identical unchanged media.
One actual playback can support several applicable conditions if their coverage is recorded
honestly; reuse earlier same-version evidence when still applicable. Changed cut/media/cues,
new neighbors, failures or missing critical evidence reopen the affected judgments. The final
encoded export still needs actual whole sound/picture review, not candidate-only substitution.

For requested cinematic quality, perform the [photographic quality gates](../creation/cinematic-look.md)
alongside detail/continuity checks. Compare with reference-backed look targets, not merely an earlier
acceptable remake. Inspect light/exposure, space/focus, materials/skin, actor-environment integration,
motion texture and processed final-size detail. A file-resolution pass, correct cast or complete
dialogue does not establish this pass. Preserve the agreed expressive response through its chosen mechanism; source acting/effect forms bind where explicitly retained. Do not neutralize a key attraction to simplify finishing.

Use applicable conditions from the scene/look and sound plan, with concrete failures rather than a
single cinematic score:

| Condition | Evidence to inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Art/material integration | Costume construction, prop scale, surface response and actor/environment contact in the adopted view |
| Physical performance | Support/weight transfer, grip/contact, consequence and settling through the required action phases |
| Attention/geometry | Necessary actors/props remain readable; eyelines, axis and light agree with world/camera geometry |
| Scene life and attention | Planned environmental activity and listener behavior persist; contact has causal feedback and settling; secondary motion does not steal focus. Intentional stillness matches the accepted scene rather than accidental background freezing or looping. |
| Sound realization | Planned voice/contact/off-screen/atmosphere/music/quiet cues are actually heard with intended timing and perspective |
| Representation/semantic color | Screen/memory/graphic/stylized layers keep their declared scale, geography and identity; information-bearing colors remain on assigned objects |

Keep static, motion, boundary and finished-scene results distinct. A correct pose or contact frame
does not prove a continuous physical action; matching faces do not prove matching materials. Broad
source comedy remains a target, not an artifact to smooth away. Critical failures return to their
art, shot/input, H3 or finishing owner before final acceptance.

1. **Single shot:** inspect the full candidate, identify usable intervals, and compare the selected
   interval with its required action, identity/look, scale, entry/exit state and performance. Reject
   unexplained smiles, missing reactions and prop substitutions even if dialogue is correct.
   Also compare source-specific gait, eye/brow/mouth sequence, body/contact phases, expressive
   intensity and reaction readability. A permitted alternative action needs experience evidence,
   not merely plot equivalence or an explanation of model feasibility.
2. **Pair playback:** play the proposed cut with its adopted predecessor at intended speed and sound.
   Check space, eyelines, action phase, emotional cause, speaker and acoustic continuity. Check the
   successor too if it already exists. A first shot has no predecessor; record that as not applicable.
3. **Scene playback:** watch end to end without production notes. Conflict, relationship changes and
   reversal must read from picture and sound. Check speech timing, listening reactions, background
   presence, sound perspective and motivated changes of light or costume.

A single-shot pass can authorize provisional editing. A single-shot plus required boundary pass can
accept that version/interval for dependent generation. An autonomous_finish plan may also release execution with honest provisional artistic status and version-bound diagnostics/repair/limitations; actual adopted exit facts remain necessary. Neither is final scene acceptance. Do not
wait for a nonexistent future shot to finish an upstream boundary check; mark the remaining scene
review pending. Final delivery needs the assembled scene/episode pass and inspection of the export —
[post-finishing](post-finishing.md) owns the needed repairs and destination-specific delivery QC.

Use time-addressed viewing evidence. A contact sheet establishes visible sampled states; ASR helps
locate words. Neither proves continuous performance, lip sync or the audible transition. If playback
or audio inspection is unavailable, preserve the candidate and mark that judgment unverified; continue available production, edit, sound and export under [autonomous delivery](autonomous-delivery.md), without a user permission request.

## Check the whole commissioned work

Close [title timing/design/integration](title-design.md#实际剪辑与出口验收) in the same whole-cut playback.
Compare source/authored function and its surrounding event, not just the title spelling. A late title
can be intended; an unexplained move that erases an opening payoff is not equivalent. Inspect the
accepted lettering in the actual background, with listener/prop/subtitle visibility and sound/read
time. Technical readability and successful animation do not by themselves establish cinematic taste.

During the existing full scene/episode/export playback, also inspect the [whole-work conditions](../creation/source-deconstruction.md#从解构承接到制作和成片)
at their actual scope: viewing promise, causal/relationship/cost development, information order,
setup/payoff, motivated grammar changes and ending residue. All EX units passing in isolation
does not establish that their arrangement still works. Serial acceptance covers only commissioned
episodes and available dependencies; unseen future payoff remains pending, not invented.

For selected improvements, compare the actual before/after mechanism at its meaningful phase
and the standalone target sequence. Record realized gain, lost attraction, unresolved interpretation
or actual feedback; a prettier insert or rationale does not establish the intended gain. Repetition
and pauses are judged by their function. Same-version playback can support these conditions too;
there is no additional fixed viewing pass, beauty score or reviewer agent.

## Keep an adoption record that can be invalidated

Attach the quality condition, reference evidence/version, observed static/motion/finishing result,
source/adopted interval and repair owner. Critical failed/unverified cinematic conditions block final
cinematic acceptance, while provisional previews can report remaining gaps. If audio cannot be heard
or movement cannot be inspected, do not label full professional audiovisual quality verified. These
judgments require media evidence and are not awarded by the structural trace checker.

For each assigned source detail record: source interval/evidence, adopted output interval/evidence,
observed description and status (matched / weakened / missing / intentionally_changed / unverified),
function/form/effect comparison and the disposition. Intentionally_changed requires the scoped
before/after decision; a label alone is insufficient. Critical weakened/missing/unverified target
conditions block final acceptance. Authorized form changes can pass through their approved target
mechanism and actual experience evidence; another condition's gain cannot compensate. A detail
that appears only outside the adopted interval is missing
from the film. Lowering the target requirement after seeing a failure reopens the creative decision;
it does not retroactively turn the failure into a match.

Put these fields beside the existing candidate/shot/edit record, not in a competing ledger:

| Field | What it records |
| --- | --- |
| Candidate | Exact file/Run Output and version or SHA-256 |
| Usable and adopted interval | Source in/out time, timebase/fps when frame precision matters, visible reason for the edit |
| Input provenance | Identity/look/layout versions, accepted predecessor and source time of any extracted reference |
| Actual state | Observed entry/exit facts, deviations and their disposition |
| Viewing evidence | Who/what reviewed which interval, evidence path/time, single/pair/scene result and pending checks |
| Decision | Rejected, provisional, accepted for continuation, or accepted in scene; required repair if any |

When candidate bytes, a cut point, predecessor, dialogue or relevant asset changes, reopen the affected
checks. Preserve unaffected candidates. Never carry acceptance forward solely because the filename
or provider task ID stayed the same. The next shot inherits the adopted out state, not a discarded
tail. Review a repaired boundary again in context.

Use [acceptance evidence](acceptance-evidence.md) to record the actual file/version, interval,
inspection method and concrete findings in the existing adoption/EDIT/DELIVERY records. No separate
plan/review snapshot or conditions ledger is needed. Continue repairs within the authorized scope;
report remaining failures or unverified modalities precisely.
